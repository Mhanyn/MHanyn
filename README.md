## 🔬 Research Projects

### 🧬 Few-Shot Learning for Rare Kidney Disease Detection *(Thesis — 2025–2026)*
> *Filling a clinical AI gap: the first joint evaluation of episodic meta-learning + Bayesian uncertainty + spatial explainability on a multi-centre CT cohort*

- **Built** a 2.5D Prototypical Network trained episodically on **KiTS23** (599 cases, 123 institutions)
- **Achieved** consistent accuracy scaling from **1-shot → 10-shot** with inference **< 100ms** on a single GPU
- **Integrated** Monte Carlo Dropout for epistemic uncertainty — calibrated confidence estimates that **flag ambiguous cases for human review**
- **Stack:** Python · PyTorch · MONAI · Prototypical Networks · Monte Carlo Dropout

---

### 🧠 Alzheimer's Disease Diagnosis & Staging *(2025)*
> *Multi-class staging from structural MRI — where calibration, explainability, and architecture search had rarely been co-evaluated*

- **Achieved 93% accuracy and macro F1 of 0.93** on 4-stage Alzheimer's classification from 6,400 MRI scans
- **Outperformed** ResNet50 and EfficientNetB0 transfer learning baselines with a custom domain-specific architecture
- **Generated** Grad-CAM hippocampal & ventricular activation maps aligned with established Alzheimer's neuropathology
- **Stack:** Python · TensorFlow/Keras · Custom CNN · ResNet50 · EfficientNetB0 · Grad-CAM
